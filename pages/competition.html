结论：竞品普遍「检测强但看不懂」或「简单到没用」，「健康解读 + 个性化方案 + 联动处理」这个组合是空白。我们的差异化不在「更准」，而在「更懂 + 更好用」。 Rivals are either “accurate but opaque” or “simple but useless”; the “interpretation + personal plan + linkage” combination is unclaimed. We differentiate not on “more precise” but on “more understanding + more usable”.

两个最值得警惕的竞品Two rivals to watch

净水器品牌 App（生态替代者）Brand app (ecosystem substitute)

威胁：已拥有家庭用户与自家设备数据，若推出「健康解读」会直接覆盖我们部分场景。Threat: owns users + device data; adding interpretation would overlap us.

机会：封闭生态不解决多品牌/通用水质；缺「画像→方案」深度；便携场景是其天然盲区。Opportunity: closed, no multi-brand; shallow personalization; portable use-cases are its blind spot.

消费级 TDS 笔（入门替代者）TDS pen (entry substitute)

威胁：极低价格、认知已普及（「测水质就买 TDS 笔」）。Threat: dirt cheap, widely understood.

机会：它停在「报数字」，恰好放大「看不懂/不会处理」的痛点——TDS 笔用户是潜在升级客群。Opportunity: it stops at numbers, amplifying our “interpretation” angle — TDS users are our upgrade pool.
