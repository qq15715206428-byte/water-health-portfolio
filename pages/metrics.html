数据指标体系与埋点设计Metrics & Analytics Design

从产品目标反推「可衡量的指标」，再落到「埋哪些点」。让决策有据可依，而不是凭感觉迭代。 From goals back to measurable metrics, then to what to instrument. Decisions driven by data, not instinct.

北极星指标（North Star）North Star Metric

每周主动查看水质并采纳健康建议的活跃用户数（Weekly Active Adopters, WAA）Weekly Active Adopters — users who actively view water quality AND adopt advice (WAA)

为什么是它：它同时衡量两条核心价值——「看得见」（主动来看，而非被推送才看）与「用得上」（采纳了建议，说明翻译层真正产生了行动）。只看 DAU 会把「打开 app」误当成功；WAA 把「打开」升级为「采纳」。It measures both core values: “see” (active viewing, not pushed) and “act” (adopting advice means the translation layer drove action). DAU mistakes “opened the app” for success; WAA upgrades “open” to “adopt”.

每一级都是前一级的子集；漏斗的「泄漏点」就是产品要优化的地方。Each tier is a subset of the last; the leak points are where the product must improve.

埋点事件表Event tracking table

埋点原则：事件驱动（以「用户做了什么」为单位）、最小必要（只埋能回答指标的）、隐私优先（只上传行为与脱敏统计，不上传原始健康画像/水质读数）。Principles: event-driven, minimal, privacy-first (behavior + anonymized stats only — never raw health profile or water readings).

当前阶段说明（诚实边界）Current stage (honest boundary)
