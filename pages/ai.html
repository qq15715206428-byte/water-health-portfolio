三个用 AI 协作的真实案例Three real AI-collaboration cases

案例 1 · 数据源抽象解耦硬件与软件Case 1 · decouple HW from SW

问题：硬件是最大不确定性，若软件依赖硬件才能跑，交付会被卡死。决策：定义 DataSource 接口，AI 并行实现 Mock/HTTP/MQTT 三套，config.js 一键切换。结果：硬件没落地，软件就能完整演示迭代、软著可独立登记。Problem: hardware is the biggest unknown; coupling SW to it blocks delivery. Decision: a DataSource interface, AI implementing Mock/HTTP/MQTT in parallel. Result: SW demos and iterates before hardware; copyright registered independently.

案例 2 · 前后端「同一领域模型」同步维护Case 2 · one domain model, two places

问题：三档阈值与「水质-健康」引擎前后端各实现一次，改一处漏一处就出 bug。决策：阈值收敛到两份 config.js 的 METRICS，写进项目规范让 AI 每次修改都同时改两份。结果：前后端一致，冒烟测试跨层验证。Problem: threshold logic duplicated front & back. Decision: converge to METRICS in two config.js, and codify “edit both together” into project conventions the AI follows. Result: consistency, cross-layer tests.

案例 3 · 冒烟测试作为「AI 的验收标准」Case 3 · smoke tests as acceptance criteria

问题：AI 生成代码快，但「看起来能跑」≠「逻辑正确」（阈值边界、MQTT 编解码、健康规则映射最易错）。决策：为核心逻辑定义可自动运行的冒烟测试作为「完成」的硬标准。结果：前端 24 通过 0 失败、后端通过，作为每次改动的回归门槛。Problem: AI code is fast but “looks runnable” ≠ correct (threshold edges, MQTT encode/decode, rule mapping). Decision: automated smoke tests as the hard bar for “done”. Result: 24 pass / 0 fail frontend, backend pass — a regression gate.

我学到的「AI 协作方法论」My AI-collaboration method

  1. 先给约束，再让 AI 发挥：明确架构边界、一致性规则，产出才可控。Constraints first: clear boundaries & consistency rules make AI output controllable.
  2. 验收标准前置：用可自动运行的测试定义「完成」，而不是看代码「像不像」。Acceptance criteria up front: tests define “done”, not vibes.
  3. 诚实区分「已实现」和「规划中」：AI 易把纸面承诺（如「机器学习」「ppb 精度」）当成已做成果，必须由产品人逐条核对——这是我踩过并纠正的坑。Honestly split “shipped” vs “planned”: AI tends to present paper promises (e.g. “ML”, “ppb precision”) as done; the PM must verify each — a mistake I caught and fixed.
  4. 图表/文档不能照搬：AI 首版只是起点，要有「重画成统一风格」的第二步。Never ship AI's first-pass visuals: redraw to a unified style as a second step.
  5. AI 有边界：纯文本模型读不了图、会幻觉、上下文有限——知道边界才知道何时换工具。Know AI's limits (can't read images, hallucinates) to know when to switch tools.

边界声明（诚实）Honesty note

本项目的产品决策、架构设计、验收标准由我（作为负责人）主导，AI 是执行杠杆，不夸大「AI 全自动完成」。软件为大创项目成果，著作权归属河海大学，AI 工具为协作方式，不影响知识产权归属与诚实性。Product decisions, architecture, and acceptance criteria were led by me as project lead; AI was an execution lever — no overclaiming of “fully automated”. The software is a college-project deliverable; copyright belongs to Hohai University; AI was a collaboration method, not a change to ownership or honesty.
