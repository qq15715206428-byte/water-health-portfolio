政策与标准Policy & standards

  • 2025 新国标《生活饮用水卫生标准》新增/收紧多项特征污染物指标 → 家庭“达标证明”诉求上升。2025 drinking-water standard tightens characteristic pollutants → rising demand for proof of compliance.
  • “健康中国”与绿色家电/以旧换新政策持续拉动净水与水质健康消费。“Healthy China” + green-appliance trade-in policies keep pulling water-health spending.

交叉验证结论：二手数据说明“市场有空间”（渗透率 23%），一手假设说明“需求具体”（三大痛点高频出现）——共同指向：用户不缺“检测硬件”，缺“从数据到健康行动的翻译与决策层”。 Cross-validation: secondary data shows market room (23% penetration); primary hypotheses show concrete needs — together pointing to the missing “translation & decision layer”, not missing hardware.
